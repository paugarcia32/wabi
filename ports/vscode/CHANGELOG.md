# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- **Wabi Dark** and **Wabi Light** colour themes.
- Warm neutrals and muted accents around a clay orange; every text colour meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) against the background.
- One flat background for the editor and the fixed parts of the window; menus and popups are lifted.
- Semantic highlighting.
